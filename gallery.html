Appearance

How to capture

  1. Start. Click the Snapshot icon or press Alt + Shift + S. Pin the icon from Chrome’s Extensions menu to keep it handy.
  2. Pick. Click an element, or drag to capture an area. ↑ and ↓ switch between an element and its parent. Press A to type exact coordinates.
  3. Keep it. The image is copied to your clipboard. Choose Save to library or Download PNG to keep it.
More tips
  • Press Esc to stop capturing.
  • Click a snapshot’s title to rename it. Renamed snapshots download under their name.
  • Choose Select, pick images, then Download to get them all in one ZIP.
  • Right-click the Snapshot icon to open this library.
  • Only what’s visible on screen is captured.
  • Settings can turn off copying, save every capture, add padding, or switch to dark mode.
  • Deleting a snapshot here doesn’t delete PNGs you downloaded or images on your clipboard. They’re separate copies.
Privacy

Everything stays in this Chrome profile. Nothing is uploaded, and the developer can’t see your images. Saved links drop query strings and login details, but page titles and the images themselves can still be private. Uninstalling Snapshot deletes the library, so download anything you want to keep first.
