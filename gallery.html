More tips
  • Turn off automatic copying, or turn on automatic saving, in Settings.
  • The initial notification hides after five idle seconds, pausing while hovered or focused. Its × hides it immediately while capture mode stays on. Dismissing it leaves the clipboard and saved library images unchanged. Download saved images from the library later.
  • Right-click the Snapshot icon to open your library.

Only the visible portion is captured. Download any images you want to keep before uninstalling; removing the extension deletes its local library.

Privacy and source links

Images stay local. New captures remove URL credentials, query strings and fragments; local-file paths are not saved. Some source links may lose their route. Page titles, paths and screenshot pixels can still be sensitive. Clipboard contents, downloads, shared screenshots and browser-profile backups are separate copies. See the privacy guide for details.
